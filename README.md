#### Now playing

**[Daily Scatter](https://github.com/peterselj/dailyscatter)** — a daily category game my family plays
on our phones. One letter, five categories, three minutes. Matching answers cancel out, results reveal
when everyone's done (or at midnight), and the day's standings post themselves to our WhatsApp group.
Includes a timer-free "helper mode" so everyone in the family can play. No backend, no build step, no
dependencies. [Play it](https://peterselj.github.io/dailyscatter/)

**Contest Bot** — the Discord bot that runs the community around Doodle Board, our VR whiteboarding app.
Moderators approve player art with an emoji reaction and the bot cross-posts it into the monthly contest
forums, credits the player, catches duplicate uploads, and gives the team ban tools and alerts for app
errors. *(Private repo.)*

#### Job Search Coaching Kit

Three tools I give my job-search coaching clients, one for each part of the hunt:

| | Tool | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **[JobSnipe](https://github.com/peterselj/jobsnipe)** | Checks your target companies' job boards every hour and emails you the moment a role that fits goes up, so you're one of the first applicants. [Try the demo](https://peterselj.github.io/jobsnipe/?demo) |
| 2 | **[JobBoard](https://github.com/peterselj/JobBoard)** | A referral-first CRM that runs the search like a sales pipeline: each opportunity gets a realistic offer probability, so you see your *expected offers* and what to do today. Your data stays private in your browser. [Live app](https://peterselj.github.io/JobBoard/) |
| 3 | **[ResumeMachine](https://github.com/peterselj/resumemachine)** | Tailors your resume to any job posting using the AI assistant you already have (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini). Nothing to install. [Set it up](https://peterselj.github.io/resumemachine/) |
